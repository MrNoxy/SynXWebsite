Automix (beat-grid) finds each song's tempo and downbeats, then triggers the blend on a phrase boundary of the current song and starts the next one on its own downbeat — stretched to the same tempo so the bars (and even vocal lines) stack like a DJ set. It trims the dead tail and the next song's intro up to its first downbeat. Best on steady-tempo music; songs with no clear beat fall back to a smooth crossfade.
