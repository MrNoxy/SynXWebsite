Set up a new device
A brand new Speedy Tracker board broadcasts its own WiFi hotspot the moment it powers on, named something like Speedy-7F2A. No display on the device, no password to type — you'll connect to it directly.
Look for a network starting with Speedy- and join it. It needs no password.
Which network did you join?
Now finish setup on the device itself — it serves its own page for entering your real WiFi network.
Pick your home WiFi (or your phone's personal hotspot) and enter its password right there on the device's page — that password stays on the device, Speedy never sees it. Then tap Connect on that page.
Almost done — reconnect your phone to your normal WiFi (the device's setup hotspot turns itself off once it has your network), then tap below to finish pairing.
Your WiFi password is entered on the device's own page and stays on the device — it's never sent to Speedy or stored in your account.
